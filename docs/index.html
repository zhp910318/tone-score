Text tone score

Paste a text you're about to send. Scored on neediness, warmth, confidence, clarity and length fit — entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

—

    This demo runs the open-source tone-score library (MIT). It is a heuristic — it scores patterns, not meaning, and it is English-only. For a full tool that also drafts replies, see the tone checker on Love Strategist.
